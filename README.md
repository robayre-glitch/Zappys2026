# Zappy Awards 2026 -- Submission Helper

A markdown file you drop into any AI tool to draft a strong Zappy Awards submission.

## What this is

The [Zappy Awards](https://zapier.com/zappy-awards) recognize the best AI builders using Zapier. Ten categories. $5,000 cash prize per winner. Published story on zapier.com. Live recognition at ZapConnect (Sept 23, 2026).

This file is an AI-powered submission coach. It knows the judging rubric, interviews you about your work, and helps you draft a submission that hits at the Transformative tier.

## How to use it

1. Copy the contents of [`submission-helper.md`](./submission-helper.md) -- or use the [raw link](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/robayre-glitch/Zappys2026/main/submission-helper.md)
2. Paste it into your AI tool of choice (Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, Zapier Agents -- anything)
3. Say: **"Help me build my Zappy Awards submission."**
4. The AI interviews you about your work, scores you against the rubric, and drafts everything you need for the form

## What it produces

- A 150-word description of your work
- Answers to all four rubric-aligned form questions
- 2-3 metrics with before/after
- A category recommendation
- A pull quote for the published story if you win

## The judging rubric

Submissions are scored on four pillars:

| Pillar | What judges look for |
|---|---|
| **Mindset** | AI as a thought partner, not a tool. Iteration, pushing back, teaching others. |
| **Strategy** | Picking the right problems. Knowing when to use AI and when not to. |
| **Building** | Shipping real systems -- durable, adopted by others, with visible artifacts. |
| **Accountability** | Defining "good" before you start. Monitoring. Owning outcomes. |

Winners score at the **Transformative** tier: work that redesigns how a role, team, or company operates with AI.

The rubric is derived from [Zapier's AI Fluency Rubric](https://zapier.com/blog/raising-ai-fluency-bar-in-hiring/) -- the same framework used to assess every Zapier hire.
